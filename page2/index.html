Our Services

HKO Construction Sdn. Bhd. is involved in several areas of Malaysia's construction industry. Its registered areas of specialization include building construction, civil engineering, and mechanical and electrical work.

Construction projects require cooperation between different professionals and technical teams. From the early planning stage to the completion of a project, contractors need to coordinate construction work, materials, equipment and workers while maintaining safety and quality standards.

01 Building Construction

Building construction is one of the major areas of the construction industry. It involves the development of structures such as residential, commercial and other types of buildings.

A construction project normally involves several stages, including site preparation, structural construction, installation of building systems and finishing work. Proper coordination is important to ensure that the different stages can be completed efficiently.

02 Civil Engineering

Civil engineering construction is another important part of the construction industry. It can involve infrastructure, structural and engineering-related construction work.

Civil engineering projects often require careful planning because they can involve large construction sites, specialised equipment and different types of materials. Engineers and construction teams need to work together throughout the project.

03 Mechanical & Electrical

Mechanical and electrical systems are important components of modern construction projects. These systems help buildings and facilities operate safely and efficiently.

Mechanical and electrical construction can involve equipment, building systems and technical installations. Proper installation and coordination are important because these systems need to work together with the overall building structure.
