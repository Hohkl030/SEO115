About HKO Construction

HKO Construction Sdn. Bhd. is a construction company based in Selangor, Malaysia. According to publicly available contractor information, the company has been registered with the Construction Industry Development Board (CIDB) since 25 July 2012 and is registered as a Grade G5 contractor.

The company provides construction-related services covering building construction, civil engineering, and mechanical and electrical work. Its building specialization includes B04, which refers to building construction work. In civil engineering, the company is registered under CE21 for civil engineering construction. Its mechanical and electrical specialization includes M15 for various mechanical equipment.

Construction companies play an important role in Malaysia's economic and infrastructure development. Construction projects require careful planning, skilled workers, appropriate materials, safety management and quality control. A reliable contractor therefore needs to coordinate different areas of work to ensure that projects can be completed properly.

HKO Construction represents one of the many contractors participating in Malaysia's construction industry. Its CIDB registration and G5 classification provide information about its registered status and areas of specialization. Through construction and engineering services, companies such as HKO Construction contribute to the development of buildings and infrastructure in Selangor and surrounding areas.
